Insertion order : Banana Apple Mango Cherry Apple Kiwi 
LinkedHashSet   : Banana Apple Mango Cherry Kiwi 
HashSet         : Apple Kiwi Cherry Mango Banana 

LinkedHashSet keeps a doubly-linked list through its entries, so iteration
follows insertion order (duplicate "Apple" did not change its position).
HashSet iterates in hash-bucket order, which is not predictable.
